# Bible Present Mobile

A web app for the iPad (or a phone): prepare lists of Bible passages and sermon quotes anywhere, and present them through **Bible Present** on a laptop.

- **Bible:** SV, KJV and LSG. Works offline once it's on the home screen.
- **Sermons:** find a sermon by code, title or place, and search the text of all sermons (via search.messagehub.info). Searching needs internet; sermons you have opened stay available offline.
- **Lists:** several named lists, kept on the device. Drag items by their grip (⠿) to change the order.
- **Connect to laptop:** on the same Wi-Fi as a laptop running Bible Present (with *iPad* switched on there), the app becomes the presenting console:
  - you see the laptop's service list and what is live,
  - you go live, step with Next/Previous, and blank,
  - you add verses and sermon paragraphs on the spot.

  *Back to my lists* brings the list back, including any changes made in church.
- **Without Wi-Fi to the laptop:** send the list as a file (AirDrop, mail…) and open it in Bible Present with *Service list ⋯ → Open list…*

## Putting it on the iPad
1. Open https://jwsw.github.io/BiblePresent-Mobile/ in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. From now on, open it from the home screen icon. It opens full screen, like an app.

Lists are kept in the app on the iPad. Send important lists to the computer now and then as a backup.
